## Work in progress

### **Family Stability under Economic Sanctions: A Cross-Country Synthetic Control Study**

*Joint work with Alireza Oryoie*

**Abstract:** This paper examines the effect of international sanctions imposed on Iran between 2008 and 2012 on marital instability. Using the synthetic control method, we construct a doppelgänger for the country from a weighted combination of countries that were not subject to similar sanctions. By comparing the post-treatment path of Iran's marital instability indicators, including the crude divorce rate, general divorce rate, and divorce-to-marriage ratio, with those of its synthetic counterpart, we aim to asses the impact of these sanctions.
