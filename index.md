## About Me

[Durōd](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D8%AF%D8%B1%D9%88%D8%AF)! I am Mohammadamin Firouzi, a master's student in Economics at the University of Tehran. My research interests include family economics, gender economics, economic history, and micro-econometrics. My current research focuses on the relationship between economic sanctions and divorce in Iran, under the supervision of Prof. [Alireza Oryoie](https://sites.google.com/view/oryoie).

Before UoT, I received my bachelor's degree in Economics, with a specialization in Quantitative Economics, from the National University of Iran.
